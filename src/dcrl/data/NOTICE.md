# Notice: third-party data

## stuttgart_sv_employment.csv

- Content: number of companies and social-insurance-subject employees by city district
  (Stadtbezirk) and economic sector (Wirtschaftsabschnitt), Stuttgart, 2020.
- Source: "Unternehmen und sozialversicherungspflichtig Beschäftigte", published on
  https://opendata.stuttgart.de/dataset/37edbf88-cec4-44fe-b5cf-e1ecc7ee87e6 and listed by
  Open Data Baden-Württemberg.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  http://dcat-ap.de/def/licenses/cc-by/4.0
- Changes: the file is re-encoded from Windows-1252 to UTF-8 by the loader, the year column
  is dropped (it is constant), and an id column is added in source row order.
- The licence is taken from the dataset's resource metadata. Check the dataset page for
  the publisher's exact wording before a public release.
